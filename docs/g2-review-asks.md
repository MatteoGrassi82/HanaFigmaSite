## Rules

- **Don't coach the wording.** G2 prohibits it, reviewers resent it, and coached
  reviews read as coached. The specific detail in each email above is a memory
  jog, not a script.
- **Don't offer your own gift cards.** Incentives are allowed only through G2's
  official program, where G2 administers the reward and labels the review as
  incentivized. Doing it yourself gets reviews removed.
- **Tell them not to include patient details.** Clinicians reach for a case
  example by instinct; a public review is not the place. Worth one sentence if
  you're on a call with them.
- **Flag the employer question.** Several of these people work somewhere with a
  policy on endorsing vendors publicly. Better they check first than retract later.
- **One note on Archie's framing:** his existing quote leans on "RPM". Don't
  correct him — a third party's characterization is his to make, and steering it
  would be worse than leaving it. Just don't quote it back into first-party
  marketing copy. See the guardrail in `docs/listing-kit.md`.

## Where the other four come from

Six warm asks converting at roughly 60% gets you to four. The remaining six come
from current customers over a quarter or two — ask at renewal, after a good
month's numbers, or when support solves something well. Put the G2 link in the
footer of your customer-facing emails and it accumulates without asking.
