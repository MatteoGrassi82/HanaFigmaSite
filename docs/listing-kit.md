## 5. Differentiators

- **vs. patient portals and apps** — voice reaches the patients who never open an
  app. 85% engagement against 15–20%.
- **vs. offshore call centers** — scales without headcount, and every call is
  documented consistently rather than variably.
- **vs. general-purpose AI voice tools** — reads the chart, follows a
  clinician-built protocol per condition, escalates to a human on defined thresholds.
- **vs. point solutions** — one platform across intake, follow-up, chronic care and
  specialty programs, rather than a tool per workflow.

## 8. Claims guardrail — read before pasting

These are not style preferences. The site was corrected in July 2026 for a reason.

**Never write:**
- "Device-less remote patient monitoring" or "device-less RPM" — RPM codes
  (99453/99454/99457) require an FDA-defined device that transmits automatically.
  Patient self-report does not qualify, and the DOJ's first RPM False Claims
  settlement was exactly that fact pattern.
- Anything implying HANA generates billable clinical time: "auto-bills",
  "20 minutes tracked", "generates the claim".
- "SOC 2 certified" or "SOC 2 compliant" while the audit is in progress.

**Write instead:**
- Device-free programs are CCM, APCM, BHI and ACCESS. On RPM, HANA is the
  engagement layer that keeps the customer's existing devices transmitting.
- HANA prepares structured documentation; a named clinician supplies and attests
  to the billable time.

If a directory form has a rigid "RPM" category checkbox, tick it only where the
listing also lets you describe the role as an engagement layer over devices.

## 10. The gap the directories won't close

A search for *"best AI voice agent patient engagement software for clinics 2026"*
returns competitor-published listicles — Retell AI, Assort Health, DoctorConnect,
OmniMD, Rasa, Parakeet, Tile Healthcare — and no HANA. Those pages are what AI
engines quote when a buyer asks the category question.

Directory listings help. The thing that actually competes is publishing the
comparison yourself. `/state-of-ai` already maps 247 companies in the category and
